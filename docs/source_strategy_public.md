# Public source-selection strategy

This is a method description, not a list of sources. Actual publishers, titles, URLs, platform identifiers, discovery queries and observed comment phrases remain private.

## Select discussions, not title coincidences

Prefer active, opinion-rich discussion formats: interviews, public-issue debates, podcasts and news or community discussions. A topic keyword in a movie, song, scripted comedy or fictional character name is weak discovery evidence. A source may be suitable for discovery while most of its comments remain irrelevant.

Keep three search-term roles distinct: strong category signals, contextual/discovery vocabulary and weak ambiguous vocabulary. Support Uzbek Latin and Cyrillic, apostrophe variation, transliteration and observed spelling variation. Expand searches from language actually observed in authorized private evidence; do not invent comments or publish the private phrase bank.

## Verify before relying on identity

Record URL reachability separately from platform identity. Where available, compare returned title and publisher metadata with the private registry. Record the verification method and uncertainty. Do not infer page identity from HTTP status, guess a source address, construct a comment permalink or invent missing context.

Access only the public content authorized for the collection task. Do not bypass authentication, private-group restrictions, CAPTCHAs or other access controls. Unavailable comments and failed retrievals are limitations, not successful collection.

## Measure useful diversity

Assess distinct useful discussions and source concentration in the plausible review pool, not only the number of discovered pages. Seek different discussion contexts and publishers when one source dominates. Keep raw evidence intact; do not inflate the review queue with neutral comments to make diversity or volume look better.

Evaluate the comment's own target, harmful claim and stance. Relevant source context may support interpretation but cannot supply an abusive statement absent from the retrieved text. Counter-speech and quoted attacks require special care.

## Publication boundary

The source registry, source-level quality decisions, links, item IDs, factual titles and publisher names stay in the local private workspace. Source-specific maintenance rules are loaded from private configuration, not hardcoded into distributed scripts.

A technically reachable source is not automatically suitable for redistribution. Release only method documentation and empty structural templates; do not publish a sample source, shortened URL, embedded video, screenshot, QR code or reconstructable identifier as a substitute for a withheld link.
